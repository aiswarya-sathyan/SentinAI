Drop 2-3 genuine clips here and the app shows a button for each (Voice and Video modes).
They are analysed for real: nothing is canned.

Naming controls the "expected vs detected" tick shown under the result:
  real_*.wav  / human_*.mp4   -> expected AUTHENTIC
  clone_*.wav / fake_*.mp4 / deepfake_*.mp4 / tts_*.wav / swap_*.jpg -> expected SYNTHETIC

Suggested set for the stage demo (works even if the mic or camera fails):
  real_voice.wav      your own voice, 4-6 s, quiet room
  clone_voice.wav     the same sentence generated by a cloning / TTS tool
  real_face.jpg       a normal photo of a face (optional: fake_face.jpg from a face-swap tool)
Audio: wav, mp3, flac, ogg, m4a.  Video: mp4, mov, avi.  Images: jpg, png.
